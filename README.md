### The thesis I keep betting on

Most people use AI like a vending machine. Walk over, paste a task, carry the
answer back by hand. Every time. It works, and it never compounds.

```
  AI-USING              AI-NATIVE
  ────────              ─────────
  you ─▶ paste ─▶ [chat]  "write this report"
     ◀─ copy  ◀──  │               │
     every time,   ▼               ▼
     by hand  a paragraph   a finished doc
```

The jump is not a smarter prompt. It is setup: giving the system your context,
your tools, and your standard procedures so it finishes the job instead of
handing you a paragraph. Most people stay on the left because that setup is the
hard part.

That is what I build, and what I teach.
